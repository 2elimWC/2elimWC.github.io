Double-Elimination format explained

Website made by u/thanosaekk21

This simulator runs example 48-team World Cups with the double-elimination format proposed by César Rennó-Costa in his 2023 paper.

Every team starts in the Main Bracket. Winning allows them to Advance to the next round, while losing Demotes them to the Repechage Bracket.

Losing a second time while in the Repechage Bracket Eliminates them from the tournament entirely.

There are two windows for a demoted team to be Promoted back to the Main Bracket: the first is to be one of two teams with the best Goal Difference that advances out of Round 3. The second is to get one of two semi-final tickets by winning every game up to Round 7.

You can run simulations with the 2026 teams at their pre-tournament strengths, or make a custom World Cup by picking the 48 teams you want to participate at their current strengths as of September 2026.
